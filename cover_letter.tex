I have been driven by fascination throughout my life. From a young age, I was introduced to the Dell home computer with Windows XP. I clicked all the buttons and slowly, by myself, figured out the intricacies of the machine. That began a fascination with computers; by the time I was twelve, I was already building my own and beginning to learn programming. My passion, curiosity, and desire to know all and everything about computers continue to this day, as I am set to graduate with a computer science degree from UCF in May of 2027. 

For five years, I have been with Publix as an associate. My good friend Joe, who is now a CSTL, exclaimed with all of his heart what an opportunity Publix was for me. He couldn't have been more correct - I have been so grateful for Publix and the imprint it has had on my life and my character. I moved up from a Front Service Clerk to a Cashier quickly, excelling through courteous hard work. Yet, I wanted more, and was able to be promoted to the Grocery department. There, I saw the `innards' of the operation - the logistics, pallets, and the effort required to even bring out the product I scanned as a Cashier. I sensed then and now the `invisible' and `visible' software systems designed and maintained to control the grocery store operation I am so intimately involved in.

I believe I am more than qualified to start developing those systems at Publix. Firstly, I joined a volunteer-based team developing a game modification played by over 500,000 people. In this role, I implemented complex GUIs, scripted event chains, and wrote documentation in collaboration with the over one hundred people on that team. My personal video game, `A Frail Qubit,' is written in the Godot game engine with GDScript, a Python-like language. I transferred that experience into Python itself, working with NumPy implementing machine learning algorithms. I have implemented classic computer science data structures using Java, and I have worked on experimental games in the Unity game engine with C\#. I have also created various static and WordPress-based websites for personal and business use, hosted on the DigitalOcean cloud provider.

Thank you so much for the opportunity to advance my career at Publix. Please contact me by email or text if you have any further questions; I will get back to you as soon as I can. Finally, to concretely see my programming experience, please visit my GitHub (brocw).
